Auto-Crop¶

Auto-Crop uses computer vision to automatically detect the slab outline in your photo and fit the crop rectangle to it. This saves time compared to manually adjusting crop handles.

Using Auto-Crop¶

In the Add Slab or Edit Slab dialog, once an image is loaded:

  1. Configure the detection settings (see below)
  2. Click Auto Crop
  3. Mapper analyzes the image and adjusts the crop rectangle to the detected slab edges
  4. Fine-tune the result manually if needed

Click Clear to remove the detected outline overlay.

Detection Settings¶

Setting Description
Background Mode How the background is identified — choose based on your photo setup
Detail Level Controls sensitivity of edge detection. Higher detail catches more edges but may include noise
Outline Mode How the detected outline is used to set the crop rectangle

Tip

Experiment with the settings to find what works best with your photo setup. Consistent lighting and a contrasting background (darker or lighter than the stone) give the best results.

Scale to Slab Size¶

The crop is mapped to the Width and Height values you entered manually. The crop rectangle is adjusted to match the slab's aspect ratio.

Photo Station¶

If you have a calibrated photo station (consistent camera position and distance), Mapper can calculate the slab dimensions automatically from the pixel measurements.

Select Photo Station and the dimensions are computed using your saved scale factors. See Photo Station Setup to calibrate.

Tips for Best Results¶

  • Use consistent, even lighting on the slab
  • A background that contrasts with the stone color works best
  • Keep the camera at the same height and angle for every photo
  • Avoid shadows across the slab edges
  • If auto-crop misses an edge, adjust manually and note what background mode worked
